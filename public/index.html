Getting Started

From zero to first payment.

1

Pick 2–3 Platforms and Apply

Don't put all your eggs in one basket. Apply to Outlier and DataAnnotation as primary targets.

2

Pass the Assessment

Most platforms require an entrance test. Read the rubric carefully. Treat it like an exam.

3

Set Up Your Workspace

Reliable computer, stable internet (10+ Mbps), quiet environment. You'll also need to set up a payment method to get paid — see the payment methods here.

Do Your First Hours

Focus on accuracy first — speed comes naturally. After a while you'll get a feel for which tasks pay best.

5

Scale to Multiple Platforms

Once established, add a second platform. Many evaluators work 20–30 hrs/week across 2–3 platforms.

03 — Tips

Tips for Success

⚡ Speed Without Sloppiness

Many companies require at least 90% quality to keep you on a project. A high accuracy rating also unlocks better-paying tasks, one rushed hour can take ten good hours to recover from.

🎓 Claim Domain Specialisms

Degree in math, CS, law, medicine, or finance? Claim it immediately. Domain tasks pay 2–3x base rate.

💰 Track Your Earnings

Log hours and hourly rate per platform per week. Some "high-paying" tasks end up paying less than simpler ones.

🔒 Protect Your Account

Never share task content publicly. Don't use AI to generate evaluations. Account bans are how evaluators lose income.

⏰ Watch for Task Drops

Most platforms release tasks at specific times, often early morning (US time zone). The best ones go fast.

🌐 Leverage Languages

Two or more languages = massive advantage. Multilingual tasks are less competitive and pay more.

My Path

I began working in this field in 2018 as a search engine evaluator, learning how relevance, intent, context, and quality are assessed behind the scenes. My degree in Sociology became a practical advantage: it trained me to understand human behavior, cultural nuance, and the social context behind the way people search, communicate, and use technology.

From there, I moved into a wider range of online AI projects, working with AI companies as a data annotator, transcriptionist, translator, and localization specialist. Working across languages, text, audio, and culturally sensitive datasets gave me a broad view of how human judgment helps machine-learning systems improve.

In recent years, I have focused extensively on LLM evaluation, AI writing evaluation, prompt design, and prompt optimization. I compare outputs, identify weaknesses, refine instructions, and help make AI responses more accurate, useful, and natural.

Where AI Meets Design

I also work as a UI/UX designer. This allows me to contribute to AI projects from another angle: evaluating interfaces, user flows, and the clarity of AI-powered experiences—not only the quality of a model's output.

I am passionate about technology and constantly experiment with AI tools for image and video creation. This hands-on exploration keeps my perspective current and helps me understand how these tools behave in real creative workflows.

04 — Payments

Getting Paid

How you receive your money. Each platform uses different methods, here's what to expect.

PayPal

The most common payment method. Almost every platform supports it. You'll need a verified PayPal account linked to your bank.

Pros

  • ✓ Widely accepted
  • ✓ Fast transfers (1–3 days)
  • ✓ No setup fee

Cons

  • ✗ Conversion fees (2.5–4%)
  • ✗ Can freeze accounts
Create a PayPal Account →
Payoneer

Popular for non-US evaluators. Virtual US/EU/UK bank accounts. Better exchange rates than PayPal.

Pros

  • ✓ Better exchange rates
  • ✓ Virtual bank accounts

Cons

  • ✗ Annual fee ($29.95)
  • ✗ Slow verification

Payment Tips

Set up PayPal first. It's accepted by almost every platform. Get it verified before applying.

If you're outside the US, add Payoneer. Better exchange rates. The annual fee pays for itself quickly.

Use Wise for large transfers. Earning $2,000+/month and converting currencies? Wise's real rate saves significant money.

Set aside 25–30% for taxes. You're a contractor, no one withholds for you.

Ready before the first task arrives?

Set up the right payment method now, then apply without adding another delay to your start.
